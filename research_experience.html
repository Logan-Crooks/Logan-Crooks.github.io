Research Experience

Research in experimental quantum systems, diamond color centers, nanophotonics, cryogenic instrumentation, electromagnetic simulation, and scientific software.

Research Assistant — Quantum Optical Devices Lab

Michigan State University  |  2022–Present

Electrons on Superfluid Helium with NV Sensing

I lead development of a hybrid quantum platform intended to measure electron-spin behavior above superfluid helium using near-surface nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond as local spin sensors.

  • Integrate cryogenic, optical, microwave, electrical, mechanical, and software subsystems into a unified experimental platform.
  • Develop experimental-control software coordinating confocal scanning, photon counting, microwave control, pulse sequencing, AOM operation, galvo scanning, time tagging, lock-in measurements, and filament operation.
  • Design, simulate, fabricate, and characterize microwave antennas for NV spin control and implement CW/pulsed ODMR, Rabi, T1, and Ramsey measurement workflows.
  • Develop low-noise cryogenic wiring, shielding, filtering, and repeatable superfluid-tight experimental-cell procedures.
  • Perform electron-transport measurements and investigate charging effects on insulating surfaces, including PCB and diamond interfaces.

Nickel-Vacancy Quantum Systems & Nanophotonics

My NiV work combines contributions to experimental quantum-optics systems with computational design of nanophotonic structures intended to improve photon extraction and emitter-cavity coupling.

  • Developed and integrated experimental hardware and computer-control interfaces for optical characterization and control of nickel-vacancy centers in diamond.
  • Contributed to experimental development, physical interpretation, and scientific discussion underlying NiV-center publications, including a 2025 Physical Review Letters article and a 2026 preprint.
  • Lead simulation and design of GaP-on-diamond nanophotonic structures using Lumerical FDTD and Python-driven computational workflows.
  • Explore pillars, pixelated structures, nanobeam cavities, and output-coupling structures under charge-stability and fabrication constraints.
  • Use genetic-algorithm optimization and automated parameter sweeps to study resonance behavior, field confinement, mode volume, emitter overlap, Purcell enhancement, and far-field collection.

Erbium Quantum Interconnect Materials

Led experimental work on erbium chloride materials for optical quantum-interconnect concepts.

  • Grew erbium chloride crystals through supersaturation and handled hygroscopic samples in a glovebox.
  • Performed absorption spectroscopy and worked with nonlinear optical frequency conversion to generate telecom-band light for erbium spectroscopy.
  • Contributed to REQuIEM and related quantum-interconnect presentations.
